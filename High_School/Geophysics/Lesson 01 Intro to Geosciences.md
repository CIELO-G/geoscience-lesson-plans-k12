# Lesson Plan: Introduction to Geoscience

**Grade Level:** 9th–12th Grade  
**Subject:** Science  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  

---

## OVERVIEW

This lesson engages students in discovering the broad field of geoscience. Through an interactive presentation and discussion, students will gain a foundational understanding of what geoscience is, the disciplines it encompasses, the work performed by geoscientists, and the career opportunities available within the field. Students will also be introduced to seismology and its importance in understanding Earth's processes and natural hazards.

---

## OBJECTIVES

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

- Define geoscience and identify its major areas of study.
- Explain the basics of seismology and its importance.
- Describe the work and career opportunities of geoscientists.
- Demonstrate their understanding by completing the lesson questionnaire.

---

## MATERIALS

- Introduction to Geoscience PowerPoint presentation
- Computer and projector or interactive whiteboard
- Quiz sheets/questionnaire

---

## PROCEDURE

### 1. Engage (10 minutes)

- Present the introductory PowerPoint presentation.
- Ask students the following questions:
  - What is geoscience?
  - What do geoscientists study and what kinds of work do they do?
  - What is seismology?
  - Why is seismology important, and how does it relate to physics?

### 2. Explore (10 minutes)

- Encourage students to ask questions about geoscience and the opportunities available within the field.
- Facilitate an open discussion about careers, research, and real-world applications of geoscience.

### 3. Explain (10 minutes)

- Use the presentation slides and personal experiences to help students connect with the realities of being a geoscientist.
- Highlight examples of research, fieldwork, and career pathways in geoscience.

### 4. Elaborate (5 minutes)

- Have students discuss in pairs or small groups one real-world problem that geoscientists help solve (e.g., earthquakes, volcanoes, groundwater, climate change, or natural hazards).
- Invite a few groups to share their ideas with the class.

### 5. Evaluate (10 minutes)

- Administer a short quiz or conduct an oral question-and-answer session.
- Have students write a brief reflection describing what they learned about geoscience and why it is important to society.

---

## ASSESSMENT

- Observation of student participation during class discussions and questioning.
- Short quiz or questionnaire assessing understanding of geoscience and seismology concepts.
- Reflection paragraph demonstrating comprehension of the lesson and its real-world relevance.
